FAQ

Before you get started.

If your question is account-specific, use the contact page so it can be matched to your student email.

What is Nexora Digital?

Nexora Digital is a creator learning hub focused on TikTok strategy, AI-assisted content, creator workflows, prompt resources and student learning tools.

Do I need experience?

No. The public curriculum and starter lessons are designed so beginners can begin with the foundation and move forward step by step.

What is included in the Support Program?

The program combines structured learning, practical tasks, account access, progress tracking, notes and creator resources.

How does the Prompt Book work?

Search the library, open a card to see its full prompt, then copy and adapt it for your own creator workflow.

How do student accounts work?

Student authentication uses email, password and email verification. After sign-in, Nexora returns you to the public homepage and keeps your account session available.

Does Nexora guarantee views or income?

No. Nexora teaches skills and workflows; platform performance and eligibility depend on factors outside the website's control.

Where do I enroll?

Use the Enroll page to submit your details. Access can then be matched to the email used for your Nexora account.
