FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Program, student access, prompt library, creator resources and account questions in one place.

What is Nexora Digital?

Nexora Digital is a creator learning hub focused on TikTok growth, AI-assisted content, practical creator systems, prompt resources and student access.

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners can start with account foundations and build skills step by step.

What is included in the Support Program?

The program combines structured lessons, practical tasks, a student portal, private notes and creator resources.

How does the Prompt Book work?

Each card opens a larger panel with the title, category and prompt text. You can copy the prompt and adapt it for your own workflow.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. The student portal uses Supabase authentication and includes Google login plus email/password login and sign-up.

Does the portal save progress?

Lesson completion, tasks, notes and time tracking are saved in the browser for the signed-in profile in the current static version.

Are earnings or views guaranteed?

No. Nexora teaches skills and workflows, but platform performance, eligibility and results depend on many factors outside the website's control.

Where do I enroll?

Use the Enroll page to submit your details, then connect Nexora's real support channel when it is ready.
