FREE LEARNING PATH

Six useful starting points.

Open each lesson, complete the small action, and move to the next one. No payment is required for this page.

01
FOUNDATION

Choose a clear creator direction

+

Write one sentence that explains what your account helps people watch, learn or enjoy. Keep it simple enough that a new visitor can understand it immediately.

Action: Finish this sentence: “My account creates content about ___ for people who want ___.”
02
PROFILE

Make your profile easy to understand

+

Your profile should match the content you plan to publish. Use a clear name, readable profile image and a short bio that supports your creator direction.

Action: Review your name, image and bio. Remove anything that makes the account direction confusing.
03
CONTENT

Build stronger first seconds

+

The opening should quickly show what the viewer is about to get. Start with a clear visual, question, result, problem or surprising moment that fits the video.

Action: Write three different openings for the same video idea before you record or generate it.
04
SYSTEM

Plan content in small batches

+

Instead of deciding from zero every day, prepare a small group of related ideas at once. This makes your workflow easier to repeat.

Action: Plan three posts around one topic: one helpful, one entertaining and one story-based.
05
AI WORKFLOW

Use prompts as instructions, not magic words

+

A useful prompt usually explains the subject, setting, camera or format, mood, important details and the result you want. Clear instructions make results easier to refine.

Action: Open the Prompt Book and compare the subject, scene, style and output instructions inside one prompt.
Open Prompt Book →
06
REVIEW

Learn from your own results

+

After publishing, compare similar posts and look for patterns in the opening, topic, length and viewer response. Use that information to improve the next batch.

Action: Pick your three strongest recent posts and write one thing they have in common.
